# summary

HuggingKG is a large-scale knowledge graph constructed from the Hugging Face community, specifically designed to facilitate machine learning (ML) resource management.[1] As the Hugging Face Hub has become a central collaborative platform for hosting models, datasets, and demos, the rapid growth of these open-source resources has accelerated information retrieval (IR) research.[2] However, traditional platforms have often relied on unstructured data, limiting the ability to perform advanced queries such as tracing model evolution or recommending relevant datasets.[1] HuggingKG addresses these limitations by capturing domain-specific relations and rich textual attributes, enabling more sophisticated interactions with ML resources than traditional keyword-based search methods.[1][3]
The graph is substantial in scale, comprising 2.6 million nodes and 6.2 million edges.[1] This structure allows for the integration of heterogeneous data resources, aligning with broader efforts in knowledge engineering to enhance knowledge-based services.[4] By structuring the data in this manner, HuggingKG overcomes the issues of incomplete or misleading metadata often found in standard dataset catalogs, thereby supporting more accurate and comprehensive analyses of the ML ecosystem.[3]
HuggingKG serves as the foundational resource for HuggingBench, a multi-task benchmark featuring three novel test collections for IR tasks, including resource recommendation, classification, and tracing.[1] Experiments conducted on the graph have revealed unique characteristics of the data and the derived tasks, highlighting its utility in advancing IR research within the ML domain.[1] The construction of such a graph also underscores the ongoing challenges in engineering knowledge graphs at scale and keeping pace with evolving domain knowledge.[4]
Both HuggingKG and the associated benchmark resources are publicly available to the research community.[1] This accessibility supports further exploration into how structured knowledge graphs can improve the discoverability and utility of open-source machine learning assets.[1]

# HuggingKG

HuggingKG is the first large-scale knowledge graph constructed from the Hugging Face community, specifically designed for machine learning (ML) resource management.[1] The Hugging Face Hub serves as a collaborative open-source platform hosting a vast array of models, datasets, and demos for the ML community, catering to diverse daily work needs.[2] While the rapid growth of open-source ML resources has accelerated information retrieval (IR) research, existing platforms like Hugging Face have traditionally not utilized structured representations, which limited the ability to perform advanced queries and analyses such as tracing model evolution or recommending relevant datasets.[1]
To address these limitations, HuggingKG captures domain-specific relations and rich textual attributes within the Hugging Face ecosystem.[1] The graph is substantial in scale, comprising 2.6 million nodes and 6.2 million edges.[1] By structuring the data in this manner, HuggingKG enables more sophisticated interactions with ML resources compared to traditional keyword-based search methods often found in dataset catalogs, which can suffer from incomplete or misleading metadata.[3] The construction of such a graph aligns with broader efforts in knowledge engineering to integrate heterogeneous data resources and enhance knowledge-based services, although challenges remain in engineering knowledge graphs at scale and keeping up with evolving domain knowledge.[4]
HuggingKG serves as the foundational resource for HuggingBench, a multi-task benchmark featuring three novel test collections for IR tasks, including resource recommendation, classification, and tracing.[1] Experiments conducted on HuggingKG have revealed unique characteristics of the graph and the derived tasks, highlighting its utility in advancing IR research within the ML domain.[1] Both HuggingKG and the associated benchmark resources are publicly available to the research community.[1]
