# CORRIGENDUM

# On-chip single nanoparticle detection and sizing by mode splitting in an ultrahigh-Q microresonator

Jiangang Zhu, Sahin Kaya Ozdemir, Yun-Feng Xiao, Lin Li, Lina He, Da-Ren Chen & Lan Yang

Nature Photon. 4, 46–49 (2010); published online 13 December 2009; corrected after print 8 January 2010.

The scale bars of the scanning-electron microscope images in Fig. 1 were incorrect: the scale for Fig. 1a should have been 10 $\mu$ m, and the scale for Fig. 1d should have been 5 $\mu$ m.

In Fig. 2b, the scale of the principal y-axis was incorrect and should have been $\times10^{-7}$ . The unit of the y-axis for the inset figure remains as $\times10^{-8}$ .

These errors have been corrected in the HTML and PDF versions of the Letter.
