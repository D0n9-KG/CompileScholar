## Related Works

**Fashion Outfit Recommendation and Generation**
Fashion outfit recommendation has evolved from predicting user preferences for existing items to generating novel, compatible outfits. Early approaches focused on modeling compatibility between items and user preferences, often treating these as separate tasks. The Hierarchical Fashion Graph Network (HFGN) unified fashion compatibility modeling and personalized recommendation by constructing a hierarchical graph structure to aggregate item information into outfit representations [1]. Similarly, the Personalized Outfit Generation (POG) model addressed the dual requirements of compatibility and personalization by connecting user preferences for individual items and outfits using a Transformer architecture, deployed at Alibaba’s iFashion platform [2]. More recently, the advent of AI-generated content has enabled Generative Outfit Recommendation (GOR), which aims to generate fashion images and compose them into visually compatible outfits. The DiFashion model utilizes diffusion models to achieve high fidelity, compatibility, and personalization in parallel image generation, overcoming the limitations of pre-defined outfit pools [3].

**Virtual Try-On and Image Synthesis**
Virtual try-on (VTON) is a critical application of image synthesis in fashion, allowing users to visualize clothing on themselves. Early methods like VITON used coarse-to-fine strategies to transfer clothing items onto person images without 3D information [4]. However, GAN-based methods often struggle with high-resolution details. Diffusion models have emerged as a superior alternative for high-quality synthesis. DCI-VTON leverages a warping module to guide diffusion models, preserving local clothing details during inpainting [5]. StableVITON further advances this by learning semantic correspondence between clothing and the human body within the latent space of pre-trained diffusion models, using zero cross-attention blocks to maintain fidelity [6]. The Smart Fitting Room framework integrates matching-aware virtual try-on with retrieval-based recommendation, combining generative and retrieval methods to enhance the shopping experience [7].

**Preference Optimization and Alignment**
Aligning generative models with human preferences is traditionally achieved through Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF), which is complex and unstable. Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) offers a simpler, more stable alternative by optimizing a policy directly using a classification loss, eliminating the need for a separate reward model [8]. This approach has been adapted for diffusion models. Diffusion-DPO re-formulates DPO for diffusion models using the evidence lower bound to derive a differentiable objective, significantly improving visual appeal and prompt alignment in Stable Diffusion XL [9]. D3PO addresses the high GPU memory requirements of diffusion denoising by using the relative scale of objectives as a proxy for human preference, reducing computational overhead while maintaining performance [10]. These methods provide the foundational techniques for fine-tuning generative models without task-specific reward functions, a paradigm that FashionDPO applies to the domain of personalized outfit generation.
