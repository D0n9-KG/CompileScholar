Paper B: Training Compute-Optimal Large Language Models (Chinchilla)
Published: 2022-03-29
Key result: For a fixed compute budget, smaller models trained on more tokens outperform larger models; optimal tokens-per-parameter ratio is ~20.
Conditions: Dense decoder-only transformers, compute budgets up to 5e23 FLOPs.
Limitation: Ratio derived by fitting power laws; extrapolation beyond fitted range unverified.
